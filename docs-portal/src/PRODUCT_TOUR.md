# Product Tour

These screenshots document the frozen HydroSwarm operator experience and provenance/authority interfaces. They illustrate product behavior; scientific performance claims are documented separately in [Scientific Evidence](SCIENTIFIC_EVIDENCE.md).

## 1. Localize plausible sources

HydroSwarm ranks plausible source locations while keeping the incident evidence and uncertainty visible to the operator.

![HydroSwarm source-localization view showing plausible source candidates and incident evidence](screenshots/reference-source-localization.png)

## 2. Decide whether more evidence is needed

The sampling view turns unresolved uncertainty into an explicit next-evidence decision rather than forcing a premature response.

![HydroSwarm evidence-guided sampling view](screenshots/reference-sampling.png)

## 3. Verify response options against the hydraulic network

Candidate response plans must pass exact hydraulic-network verification before they can become simulator-verified.

![HydroSwarm response-verification view showing hydraulic verification of a response option](screenshots/reference-response-verification.png)

## 4. Stop at the human approval boundary

Simulator verification does not authorize field action: approval remains a separate, explicit human decision.

![HydroSwarm approval-boundary view showing the separate human approval step](screenshots/reference-approval-boundary.png)

## 5. Inspect model and operational authority

The authority interface makes the current evidence and permission level visible instead of treating a model prediction as permission to act.

![HydroSwarm model-authority view showing model and operational authority](screenshots/model-authority.png)

## 6. Inspect validation and provenance

The validation view exposes the checks and provenance used to distinguish verified evidence from unsupported claims.

![HydroSwarm validation and provenance view](screenshots/validation.png)
